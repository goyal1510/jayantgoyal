## Decisions

- The earlier isolation decision is superseded by the final product direction: the approved redesign will replace the canonical Portfolio home while preserving useful public routes and integrations.
- The experimental app used temporary content and imagery so the first passes could focus on visual direction, interaction, and reusable structure.
- The implementation uses only effects that can be rendered and verified in the browser. AI-generated concept imagery is explicitly excluded from the app.
- The app intentionally starts with static local project data and copied screenshots; it can be connected to the database after the experience direction is proven.
